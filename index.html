Privacy Policy

Ummah Prayer Times (Android and iOS)
Effective date: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ummah Prayer Times mobile application (“the App”, “we”, “our”), published by Barkat Faridi, handles information when you use it on Android or iOS devices.

In short:

  • The App has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not run any server that receives your data, and we do not sell or share your personal information.
  • Your location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Prayer times are calculated on your device.
  • Only a few lookups (place name, elevation and time-zone check) send coordinates or a time-zone name to the services listed below.
  • Your settings are stored only on your device, unless you choose to export a backup file yourself.

1. Information the App uses

1.1 Location

The App asks for permission to access your device’s location (precise or approximate) so it can:

  • calculate accurate prayer times, sunrise and Hijri dates for where you are;
  • show the Qibla direction;
  • show the name of your current town or city;
  • choose sensible defaults for your region, such as the calculation method.

Background location (optional). If you turn on automatic location updates and grant “Allow all the time” (Android) or “Always” (iOS) location access, the App checks your location while it is in the background. It does this only to detect that you have travelled to a new place, so that prayer times, notifications and alarms stay correct without you opening the App.

  • On Android this runs as part of the App’s ongoing prayer-time notification (a foreground service).
  • On iOS the App uses the system’s “significant location change” service, which wakes it only after a substantial move.

The App works without background location. If you decline it, prayer times still update whenever you open the App. You can also enter locations (“homes”) by hand without granting any location permission.

Your location is stored only on your device, together with any homes you save. It is never uploaded to us.

1.2 Settings and preferences

Your choices, such as calculation method, Hijri date settings, saved homes, notification and alarm preferences, theme and sound settings, are stored locally on your device (and in the App’s shared storage for the iOS home-screen widget). They are not sent to us.

1.3 Motion sensors

The Qibla compass and the level indicator read the device’s magnetometer and accelerometer (on iOS, “Motion & Fitness” data). These readings are processed on your device the moment they are received, and they are not stored or transmitted.

1.4 Notifications and alarms

The App uses local notifications and exact alarms to announce prayer times, show the next prayer and play the Fajr alarm. These are scheduled on the device itself. The App does not use push-notification servers to send you messages, and it does not register your device with any messaging service.

1.5 Files you choose

Some features work only with files you select or create:

  • Backup and restore (“Move to a new phone”): the App can create a .json file that contains your settings and saved homes, including their coordinates. The file is created only when you ask for it, and you decide where it goes (email, cloud storage, messaging app, and so on). After you send it, the service you chose handles it under its own privacy policy. When you restore, the App reads only the file you pick.
  • Moon-sighting data import: the App reads only the file you select.
  • Timetable sharing, PDF and printing: monthly and Ramadan timetables are created on your device and shared only through the share or print sheet you open.

2. Third-party services the App contacts

To give accurate results, the App sends limited information to the following services. No name, email, device identifier or advertising ID is included in these requests.

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
Device geocoder: Google (Android) / Apple (iOS) Latitude and longitude, or a place name you type when you search for a home To turn coordinates into a town or city name, and to find a place you search for
Open-Meteo Elevation API Latitude and longitude To get the elevation of a location, which improves sunrise and sunset accuracy
WorldTimeAPI Your time-zone name (for example “Europe/London”) To check the current daylight-saving rules for your time zone

Like any internet request, these services can see your device’s IP address. We have no control over their data practices. Please see their own privacy policies: Google, Apple, Open-Meteo. When your device is offline, these lookups are skipped and prayer times are still calculated on your device.

3. Device backups

Your phone’s operating system may include the App’s locally stored settings in your personal device backup (Google’s Android backup or Apple’s iCloud backup), depending on your device settings. These backups are managed by Google or Apple under your account and are not accessible to us.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or any other personal identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.
  • We do not use the microphone or camera.

6. Data retention and deletion

All data the App keeps is on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App’s storage (Android: Settings › Apps › Ummah Prayer Times › Storage › Clear storage) or by uninstalling the App. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

7. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Requests to the online services listed above use encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed specifically at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the App does not send your personal data to us, you can exercise these rights directly on your device as described in section 6. If you have any questions, contact us using the details below.
